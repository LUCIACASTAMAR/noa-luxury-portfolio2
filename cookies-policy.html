Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Publication checklist: This is a draft. Confirm which cookies and third-party services are actually used and complete the table accordingly before publishing.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies can store or access information on a visitor's device. Some are necessary for the website to operate; others may be used for analytics, preferences or marketing.

2. Categories used on this website

  • Essential storage: used for basic functionality and to remember your cookie choice.
  • Analytics: intended for measuring website usage, for example with Google Analytics 4, only if it is installed and after the required consent.
  • Marketing: intended for advertising or marketing technologies, only if such tools are added and after the required consent.

At the time of drafting this page, the preference panel supports these categories, but that does not mean analytics or marketing tags are already installed. The final policy must list the actual technologies, purposes, providers, duration and cookie names after they have been verified in the live website.

3. How to choose or change your preferences

When the banner appears, you can accept all optional categories, reject optional categories, or configure them individually. You can reopen the panel at any time using the Cookie settings control. Optional analytics or marketing tags must remain blocked until consent is given where required.

4. Third-party services

If Google Analytics 4 is enabled, Google may process usage data according to the configuration and Google Analytics terms. The website owner must verify the final setup, data retention, signals and any international transfers. Other embedded or marketing services must be added to this policy if used.

5. Browser controls

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect the website's ability to remember your preferences.
